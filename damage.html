    Assumptions and limits
    • Player and wild Pokémon always use level 100, overriding dataset levels and any pasted Level line.
    • Wild Pokémon have 0 EVs and independent, uniformly random IVs from 0–31. Neutral nature is the default; you can select another nature.
    • Ancient Dungeon: wild stats ×1.65 except Speed. New Moon/Full Moon islands and Stark Mountain Entrance/Volcano/Chamber: wild stats ×1.5 except Speed. These areas give the player no stat bonuses.
    • Other areas: player ×2.5 Attack/Special Attack, ×2 Defence/Special Defence/Speed; wild ×2 HP/Defence/Special Defence. Only in these other areas, wild offensive ×1.33 multipliers are intentionally ignored.
    • Multipliers are applied after the normal stat formula and nature, rounding up for wild Pokémon and down for the player. This ordering still needs calibration against PokeNexus.
    • Wild Pokémon start at full HP. The player is at 1 HP for Flail/Reversal and full HP for other moves. No prior setup/status/hazards or enemy attacks. Entry weather/terrain is modeled. Held Mega Stones/Primal Orbs assume the transformed player form.
    • Damage-roll OHKO chance is conditional on hitting. OHKO/use includes accuracy, but not random critical hits; forced critical moves still use their normal rules. This is not a survival or one-turn battle simulator.
    • Ordinary moves enumerate all 1,024 HP/defence IV pairs. Moves/abilities needing additional random stats use 512 reproducible full-IV samples, labeled as estimates.
    • Standard multi-hit moves are included. Unsupported moves or transformations are explicitly reported, not silently treated as guaranteed kills.
    • The area average counts each distinct species equally for the selected method. Rarity is informational only. This is not an encounter-frequency-weighted probability.
